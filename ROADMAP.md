# Concordance roadmap

Ideas from the globe POC. The goal is still the same: this has to feel like **a place you could write about**, not only a noise ball.

## Now

These close the loop from “pretty planet” to “worldbuilding tool.”

1. **Climate overlays** — false-color temperature, humidity, continentalness, erosion. Tests whether biomes come from a real climate field.
2. **Click-to-pin** — click the surface, keep lat/lon, biome, elevation. Pins are the article hook.
3. **Article stub** — a side dock with a title on the selected pin. No wiki editor yet.
4. **Seed as a first-class object** — show, edit, copy, rebuild this seed, or roll a new one.
5. **View presets** — Atlas (even light), From space (lit globe), Climate (overlay).
6. **Daylight playhead** — play/pause the existing daylight slider.

## Next

7. **Named features** — peaks, basins, islands from the same noise, auto-labeled. Pins that write themselves.
8. **Minimap / unwrapped strip** — find the desert you just spun past.
9. **Coastal currents / wind** — a few vector strokes so weather feels systemic.
10. **Compare two seeds** — split view. Makes the randomiser a tool, not a slot machine.

## Later

11. **Non-spherical sea level** — oblate world or inland seas. After overlays and pins exist.
12. Full article editor, hyperlinks, auth, universes, persistence, planetary LOD.

## Out of scope (still)

Auth, multi-world UI, streaming LOD.
